# Interim conduct expectations

Status: accepted interim expectations; formal code-of-conduct adoption deferred.

InferOps has not yet adopted an externally maintained code-of-conduct framework.
Adoption is deferred until the project has a tested private reporting and response
path. This avoids promising confidential handling that the repository cannot
currently provide. The decision must be revisited before the first versioned release
or before external maintainers are added, whichever happens first.

In the meantime, contributors and maintainers are expected to:

- communicate respectfully and focus criticism on work, not people;
- welcome questions and differing technical perspectives;
- avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, and disclosure
  of another person's private information;
- respect repository scope, licensing, security, and evidence requirements;
- stop and involve a maintainer when an interaction becomes unsafe or disruptive.

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate contributions and may temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when these expectations are violated. Enforcement
decisions should be proportionate, documented privately where possible, and avoid
unnecessary disclosure about affected people.

Do not place sensitive conduct reports in a public issue. Because no dedicated
private reporting channel is currently published, confidential reporting is a known
blocker rather than an available project capability.
