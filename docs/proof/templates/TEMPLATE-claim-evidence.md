# TEMPLATE — claim and evidence record

This file is a **template**, not evidence. It records nothing and proves nothing.
Copy it to a filled record and fill it in. Do not edit this file to hold results.

Placeholders are written as `<...>`. A filled record contains none of them.

This template binds **one** public claim to the evidence for it. One claim per
record: a record covering three claims is a record whose limitations apply to
whichever of them the reader was not thinking about.

A claim also has a row in
[the claim and test matrix](../../testing/claim-test-matrix.md), naming its layers,
environment, required level, and evidence owner. This record is what that row cites.
Neither is a substitute for the other, and the matrix will not accept a citation
from a claim that is not certified.

The section headings below are required and are checked by
[`tests/telemetry/`](../../../tests/telemetry/). Add sections freely; do not remove
one, and do not repeat one.
